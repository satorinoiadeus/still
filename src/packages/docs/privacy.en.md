# Still — Privacy

Still is an independent AGPL-3.0 derivative of HamsterBase Tasks. Tasks are stored locally in your browser or app data directory. Still does not add analytics or its own hosted service.

Optional upstream cloud, self-hosted sync, attachments and AI integrations retain their existing implementations. Enabling an integration sends data to the endpoint you configure or the upstream provider. Review that provider’s terms and privacy policy before connecting. Upstream account and payment screens refer to HamsterBase services, not a Still subscription. Network service availability has not been verified for this derivative.

Export a backup before changing devices or deleting application data. Browser storage can be cleared by the browser. Keep your encryption password; lost keys cannot be recovered by Still.

Source and issue tracker: https://github.com/satorinoiadeus/still
